Accepted 2026-10-19: the extended post-fall replay is for the caregiver's eyes only; the automatic verdict still scores fall ± 10 s.

Back to top

Context

  • The replay tape is moving from ±10 s to -10 s / +60 s (EXTENDED_POST_FALL_ENABLED).
  • The hindsight classifier and its metrics (93.3% recall / 4.2% false falls on tuning, 100% / 0% holdout) were tuned and ratchet-gated on 20 s clips.
  • The browser badge and the server worker must give the same verdict for the same fall. That was the reason all windows were unified to ±10 s.

Back to top

Options

  • Score the full 70 s. More evidence (still on the floor at +40 s), but untested: 50 s of extra behaviour (getting up, bed movement) could flip verdicts. Needs a corpus re-run and retune first.
  • Keep scoring ±10 s. No retune and no verdict drift. The extra 50 s helps the human reviewer only.

Back to top

Decision

  • Keep ±10 s. The gateway trims rows in compute_and_store_verdict; the browser runs a fresh hindsight pass over the same slice (verdictWindowRows), so both sides score identical input.
  • The verdict CLI bundle is unchanged.

Back to top

Consequences

  • Verdicts are identical with the flag on or off.
  • The browser runs a second, small hindsight pass when the tape is longer than ±10 s.
  • Scoring the longer tape stays possible later as its own experiment (corpus gates, retrain loop).
