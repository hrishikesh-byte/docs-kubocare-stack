Fall replay can now show 10 s before and 60 s after a fall, opening at 5 s before the fall, behind a gateway flag that is off by default. The verdict still scores ±10 s.

Back to top

Why

  • Caregivers asked to see what happens after a fall (did the person get up, stay down), not just the 10 s after impact.
  • Before this, every layer used one ±10 s tape: replay view, archived clip, verdict.

Back to top

What changed

Uncommitted on the working branches, pending Hrishi's review: gateway dev-in-hrishikesh (base e962b80), frontend dev-fastapi-hrishikesh. Socket untouched.

Gateway

  • New flag EXTENDED_POST_FALL_ENABLED (default false) and INCIDENT_POST_WINDOW_SEC (default 60) in relay/config.py, plus helpers fall_window_ms, verdict_window_ms, auto_clip_delay_sec.
  • Auto-clip, boot backfill and GET /api/falls/{id}/replay use the -10 / +post window.
  • Auto-clip delay is raised to post window + 3 s (63 s when on), so the archived clip never misses its tail. Off: stays 30 s.
  • Replay response gains recording_complete (false until the tail is recorded).
  • compute_and_store_verdict trims rows to ±INCIDENT_WINDOW_SEC before the Node worker.
  • Two small fixes that apply in both flag states: the replay endpoint no longer persists a verdict from a half-recorded tape, and the boot backfill re-schedules a fall whose tail is not recorded yet instead of archiving a truncated clip.

Frontend

  • Replay opens at fall - 5 s using fall_time_ms from the replay response. The old fallback guessed the clip centre, which on a -10/+60 tape would open 20 s after the fall (hit the Slack/SMS magic-link view).
  • "Still recording" shown while recording_complete is false; the tape is re-fetched every 5 s (max 30 tries) and new frames are spliced in without moving the playhead. Stops on exit.
  • Browser verdict scores ±10 s with a fresh pass over that slice, same as the server worker. Verdict is deferred until the tape is complete.
  • Labels show "-10S / +60S" (or "±10S" for centred clips). Loops and replay-from-end restart at the 5 s pre-roll instead of frame 0.
  • Verdict CLI bundle not rebuilt: nothing it uses changed.

How to enable

  • Gateway .env: EXTENDED_POST_FALL_ENABLED=true (and optionally INCIDENT_POST_WINDOW_SEC). Restart the gateway.
  • No frontend flag: the frontend follows whatever tape the gateway serves.
  • Off restores today's ±10 s behaviour exactly. Clips archived while on stay 70 s and still replay fine.

Ship gate

  • Efficient and secure: no new endpoints or auth paths. Cost: clips and replay payload are 3.5x bigger (70 s vs 20 s); not measured on a real clip yet. Each refresh while recording is one replay fetch every 5 s for up to about a minute per open replay, plus a hindsight rebuild in the browser.
  • Regressions: opening position on magic-link replays (covered by test), truncated archive clips if the delay were not raised (covered), verdict drift (trimmed to ±10 s on both sides, covered by parity test). Posture near the old tail can change slightly after a refresh, since the hindsight pass smooths over neighbours.
  • Tests: gateway app/test/test_fall_window.py 7/7 (flag on and off). Frontend extendedReplayWindow.test.ts 11/11; full jest suite 264 passed, 13 skipped (corpus not on this laptop). tsc: no new errors (4 existing ones in incident screens). ESLint not installed in this checkout, not run.
  • Not tested yet: the gateway replay route end to end (no gateway venv on this laptop), corpus gates, and a live fall on kcsn0008 replayed at +15 s and +2 min.
