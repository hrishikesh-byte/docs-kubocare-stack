Back to top

Why

  • US carriers block SMS from 10DLC numbers (normal US local numbers) unless the sender has an approved brand and campaign. This is called A2P 10DLC registration.
  • Until a campaign is approved, every number in the Twilio account shows "Messaging disabled". Voice calls are not affected.
  • 10DLC only covers US numbers texting US phones. Texts to Indian phones fall under India's DLT rules, which is a separate registration.

Campaign details to submit

  • Use case: Account Notification.
  • Description: fall alerts to registered caregivers of elderly residents monitored by KuboCare sensors, with a link to a short replay.
  • Sample messages: the fall replay link from gateway/app/services/fall_sms_service.py, with a sample /r/ link on our own domain and "Reply STOP to opt out." added. Use two variants (different room and time) if the form asks for two.
  • Left out on purpose: the pause-button low-battery SMS (handle_twilio_sms in gateway/app/services/mqtt_callbacks.py). The pause button is no longer used, so that SMS never fires. Its code is still in the gateway.
  • Opt-in: the facility admin or the caregiver adds the mobile number during onboarding and turns on SMS alerts. Link a public privacy policy on kubocare.ai.
  • Embedded links: yes. Phone numbers: no.

End to end test plan

  1. Set a test caregiver's country_code to +1 and mobile to a US phone. Set resident_profile.sms_alert = true.
  2. Gateway env: FALL_ALERT_CHANNEL=sms, SMS_FALL_ALERT_ENABLED=true, and a public PUBLIC_BASE_URL (needed for the status callback and the link).
  3. Trigger a fall on kcsn0008 after redis-cli del kcsn0008_room1.
  4. Check: the SMS arrives, Twilio Messaging logs show Delivered (error 30034 means the number is not linked to the campaign), the fall_replay_sms row reaches delivered, and the link opens the replay.
  5. Reply STOP, trigger another fall: Twilio should block it with error 21610. Reply START to undo.
